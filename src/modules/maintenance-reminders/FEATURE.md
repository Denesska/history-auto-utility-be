# Maintenance due reminders

## Functionality

HAU App tells you when a car needs its next service, without you having to open the Plan. Every morning (08:05) it checks the active cars you own or have been given access to.

**Plan reminders.** For each Plan item that is tracked (engine oil, filters, brake fluid, tires, ...) and has at least one logged service linked to it, the app works out how close the next service is, exactly as the Plan page does (by distance or by time, whichever comes first). You get:
- a **"coming up"** reminder when the service is less than 14 days away about 1000 km (or less) remain, or (for items counted in oil changes, e.g. a diesel fuel filter) exactly one oil change remains;
- a **"due now"** reminder the first time the service is due;
- an **"overdue"** reminder 30, 60 and 90 days after it became due (three at most), then silence until you log a new service of that kind (which starts a fresh cycle).

Each stage is sent once. If the service is already due the first time the app sees it, you get "due now" directly instead of "coming up"; if it is already more than 30 days late you get a single "overdue" reminder instead, and if it is more than about 90 days late you get nothing (so switching the feature on does not dig up long-forgotten items). If the car's mileage hasn't been updated for more than 60 days, distance is ignored and only the date counts, so an old odometer reading never triggers a false alarm. Items you turned off in the Plan, and items with no interval, are never reminded.

**Your own reminder dates.** A reminder date on a maintenance record can carry a name of your choice (a "custom reminder", up to 80 characters); that name is used in the notification and email in place of the service category. If you set a reminder date on a maintenance record, you are notified on the days you chose in Settings ("days before", default 7, 3, 1 and the day itself) and once the day after.

Reminders arrive as an email, an entry in the in-app notifications (tap it to open that car's Plan) and a push notification on your phone/browser. Texts are in your app language (English, Romanian or Russian, with correct singular/plural day forms, e.g. "1 zi", "2 zile", "20 de zile" in Romanian, "1 день", "2 дня", "5 дней" in Russian) and name the car (your own label plus the plate), the service, and the time/distance left, e.g. "Oil change for Irina is due in 9 days / ~800 km", "... is due now", "... is overdue by 34 days". A failed email is retried over the next three days. Cars of a business whose Maintenance feature is off are skipped. Dates in emails are written with the month name in your language. Emails end with a note that you can snooze the reminder or mute the car from the notifications panel in the app.

Every reminder can be snoozed from the in-app notifications list: remind me again in 7 or 30 days, or never again for that item. A plan item stays silent until you log a new service of that item; a record reminder date is snoozed on its own. When a timed snooze ends and the item is still pending you get one reminder that day (even if it is not a scheduled day), then the normal cadence resumes. Snoozing only affects you, not other people who share the car. A whole car can also be muted (all documents, maintenance and record-date reminders) for 1, 3 or 6 months or until turned back on; when a timed mute ends there is no catch-up burst, items continue on their normal cadence (the overdue cap and offsets still apply).

Reminders can be switched off in Settings with "Notify me when maintenance is due" (on by default). The days row in Settings applies to record reminder dates.

## Implementation

- Service: `history-auto-utility-be/src/modules/maintenance-reminders/maintenance-reminders.service.ts`. `@Cron('5 8 * * *')` -> `run({ dryRun?, today? })` returns a `RunSummary` (counts + candidates; start/end log lines). Structure and send ordering mirror `document-reminders` (see its FEATURE.md): `NotificationsService.create(user, 'MAINTENANCE_DUE', data, { forcePush: true })` -> `MaintenanceReminderSent` row (`email_sent=false`) -> `MailService.sendMaintenanceDue` -> `email_sent=true`; `retryFailedEmails` re-sends only the email for rows with `email_sent=false` from the last 3 days. Shared helpers in `src/common/reminders/reminder.util.ts`.
- Plan math: `maintenance-record/plan-due.util.ts` (`computePlanDueItems`, `decideStage`) is item-based (`plan-item.catalog.ts` definitions for the car, last done = latest record linked via `MaintenanceRecordPlanItem`, progress = max of km / months / ENGINE_OIL-record count) and a pure port of the FE `shared/utils/plan-items.util.ts` (`buildPlanItems`) — keep both in sync. The job always uses the default "Normal" usage profile and the catalog intervals: the active profile/custom intervals are client-only session state, so they cannot be known server-side. The only per-user override honoured is `CarMaintenanceSetting.tracked` (per item; across the user's profiles any `false` wins, else any `true`; custom intervals ignored), which is the maintenance equivalent of dismissing (`CarDeadlineOrder.dismissed_keys` is documents-only).
- Stages: SOON (not due, `nextDueDate` <= 14 days, `kmRemaining` <= 1000 or `oilChangesRemaining` = 1; `soonBy` 'oil' when only that fired), DUE (progress >= 100%), OVERDUE. For a full bar, `decideDueAction(daysPastDue, hasDueRow)` in `plan-due.util.ts` decides: `daysPastDue` is measured from the earlier of a passed `nextDueDate` and the DUE row's `created_at` (today if neither); step = floor(daysPastDue / 30). Step 0 -> DUE once; steps 1-3 -> `OVERDUE:<step>` (so a first-seen item 40 days late gets OVERDUE:1 directly, no DUE); step >= 4 (120+ days; the third step covers days 90-119) -> nothing until a newer record starts a new cycle. SOON is skipped once a DUE row exists for the cycle.
- Stale mileage: odometer = `actual_mileage ?? current_mileage`; freshness = newest of `actual_mileage_updated_at` and the newest record that has a mileage. Older than 60 days (or none) -> odometer passed as null, decision by date only (`summary.carsWithStaleMileage` counts them).
- Dedup (`MaintenanceReminderSent`, unique `(user_id, car_id, key)`): plan keys `plan:<ITEM>:<lastRecordId>:SOON|DUE|OVERDUE:<step>` (a newer record = new cycle); record-date keys `rec:<recordId>:<daysLeft>`. The `data` Json column stores the notification payload so a failed email is retried without recomputing.
- Record dates: `MaintenanceRecord.reminder_title` (optional, max 80, trimmed; nulled whenever `expiry_date` is cleared, in `maintenance-record.service.ts`) is copied into the notification `data.reminderTitle` and wins over category/description in `subject()` of `maintenance-due.template.ts` (HTML-escaped in emails). Every `MaintenanceRecord.expiry_date` of an active car with daysLeft in the user's `expiry_reminder_days` or -1 (stage `EXPIRY`). Gated by `maintenance_reminders_enabled` only (not by the documents toggle).
- Snooze: `ReminderSnooze` rows (kind `MAINTENANCE_PLAN`, `item_key` = `<ITEM>:<lastRecordId>`, so a new service resets it; kind `MAINTENANCE_RECORD`, key = record id). `silent` items are skipped; for `ended` ones `handleSnoozeEnd` sends the item's current stage once with key `<normal key>:SNOOZE_END:<until local date>` (full send/email-retry path), sets `end_notified_at` and writes a marker row under the normal stage key so that stage is not repeated the next day. A snooze whose item is not yet SOON/DUE keeps waiting (the end reminder fires when it first becomes pending). Notification `data` carries `item` (+ derived `category`) + `recordId` (last record of the cycle) or `recordId` (record date, `stage` EXPIRY) and `snoozeEnd`.
- Car mute: `CarReminderMute`; a muted car is skipped for that user (plan items, record dates and email retries). No catch-up when a timed mute ends.
- Kill-switch: env `REMINDERS_CRON_DISABLED=true` makes the daily `@Cron` handler log "reminder cron disabled by env" and return (set in `.env.dev` only, so the shared dev DB's real accounts are not emailed; unset = enabled, prod unaffected). `run()` from scripts/admin is not affected.
- `run({ onlyUserId })` restricts recipients and email retries to one user (safe real test runs: `scripts/reminders-real-run-dev.ts <documents|maintenance|both> --user=ID [date] [--send]`, dev DB only, dry run first and aborts on foreign candidates).
- Setting: `UserSettings.maintenance_reminders_enabled` (default true; DTOs + `user-settings.service.ts`); FE toggle in `features/settings`.
- Recipients and scope: owner + accepted `CarUserAccess` users, per-recipient `CarUserNickname` label; org cars skipped when `FeatureAccessService.disabledOrgIds('maintenance')` contains the org. `MAINTENANCE_DUE` is car-bound in `NotificationsService.filterForContext`.
- Day counts in texts go through `daysText` (`mail/templates/base-email.template.ts`; also used by `document-expiring.template.ts`); the FE equivalent is `NotificationsPanelComponent.daysText` with `sidebar.notifications.daysUnit.{one,few,many}`.
- Text: `mail/templates/maintenance-due.template.ts` (email + `maintenanceDuePushText`, ro/en/ru item map for Plan reminders, category map for record dates; push wired in `notifications/push.service.ts`). In-app text: FE `notifications-panel` via `sidebar.notifications.maintenanceDue.*` / `maintenanceCategories.*`; tap -> `/cars/details/:id/plan`. Email dates are localized per recipient language through the shared `formatDate(date, lang)` in `mail/templates/base-email.template.ts` (Intl long-month format; en -> en-GB, ro -> ro-RO, ru -> ru-RU, unknown codes passed straight to Intl, falling back to en-GB). Emails carry a short footer hint (`snoozeHintHtml`) pointing to in-app snooze/mute; there is deliberately no snooze link in emails. Push texts contain no dates.
- Dry run: `POST /admin/maintenance-reminders/dry-run` (admin only, body `{ today?: ISO date }`) or `ENV_FILE=.env.dev npx ts-node -T scripts/maintenance-reminders-dry-run.ts [YYYY-MM-DD]` (aborts unless `DATABASE_URL` contains `hau_db_dev`). Neither sends or writes anything. Because a dry run writes no DUE row, OVERDUE steps cannot be simulated by it.
- Known gaps: no catch-up for missed days (SOON/DUE are re-evaluated daily, so they self-heal; record-date offsets do not); at rollout only items due within the last ~120 days notify; Records without any linked Plan item never feed a Plan reminder.
